Islandwide Wonders

Explore Sri Lanka’s Destinations

"From ancient royal kingdoms and pristine golden coastlines to misty tea valleys and wild elephant sanctuaries."

Category 01

Historical & Cultural Heritage

Step into thousands of years of royal history, UNESCO ruins, and sacred epicentres.

Ancient Kingdom

Anuradhapura

UNESCO Sacred City • Stupas & Monasteries

First ancient capital of Sri Lanka, home to colossal white stupas, sacred Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi tree, and intricate stone carvings.

Medieval Capital

Polonnaruwa

Royal Palaces • Gal Vihara Sculptures

The second royal capital showcasing well-preserved palace ruins, royal baths, and masterfully carved rock-cut Buddha statues.

Wonder of World

Sigiriya

5th-Century Lion Rock • Sky Fortress

Iconic granite rock fortress built by King Kasyapa, boasting ancient water gardens, mirror wall, and heavenly frescoes.

Cave Monastery

Dambulla

Royal Rock Cave Temple • Murals

Largest and best-preserved cave temple complex in Sri Lanka, featuring five rock sanctuaries filled with over 150 Buddha statues.

Our HQ Base

Kandy

Temple of Tooth • Sacred Hill Capital

The last royal capital nestled amidst scenic hills and lake, housing Sri Dalada Maligawa and traditional cultural dance heritage.

Northern Realm

Jaffna

Nallur Kovil • Dutch Fort & Islands

Distinctive northern peninsula offering rich Tamil culture, vibrant Hindu kovils, historic fort, and secluded islands like Nainativu.

Legendary Trail

Ramayana Trails

Ashok Vatika • Seetha Amman Temple

Sacred pilgrimage route connecting legendary sites from the ancient epic Ramayana including Hakgala, Munneswaram, and Sanjeevani hills.

Category 02

Coastal & Beach Destinations

Sun-drenched tropical shores, coral reefs, surfing havens, and colonial coastal forts.

East Coast

Trincomalee (Trinco)

Nilaveli Beach • Pigeon Island Snorkeling

World's finest natural harbor, turquoise waters of Nilaveli, cliffside Koneswaram Kovil, and marine life at Pigeon Island.

Shallow Bay

Pasikuda

Calm Barrier Reef • Luxury Resorts

Famous for its shallow outer reef allowing visitors to walk hundreds of meters into crystal-clear turquoise waters safely.

South Coast

Mirissa

Blue Whale Safaris • Coconut Tree Hill

World-class whale watching hub, crescent-shaped sandy bay, iconic palm groves, and vibrant evening beach dining.

Surf & Culture

Weligama

Beginner Surfing • Stilt Fishermen

Broad sandy bay famous for gentle waves ideal for surf learners, Taprobane Island, and traditional stilt fishermen.

North-West Island

Mannar

Baobab Trees • Adam's Bridge Causeway

Remote windswept island island home to ancient Arabian baobab trees, flamingos, Portuguese fort, and Adam's Bridge sandbanks.

South-West Coast

Hikkaduwa

Coral Sanctuary • Sea Turtles

Lively beach town famous for wild sea turtles coming right up to the shoreline, coral reef snorkeling, and surfing.

Kitesurf Capital

Kalpitiya

Lagoon Kitesurfing • Dolphin Pods

Coastal peninsula renowned as South Asia's premier kitesurfing destination and home to massive pod schools of spinner dolphins.

Airport Gateway

Negombo

Dutch Canals • Lagoon Fish Market

Convenient coastal town located 15 minutes from international airport, rich in colonial Dutch canal history and fish markets.

World Surf Point

Arugam Bay

Right-Hand Point Break • Elephant Safaris

World-famous surfing mecca on the southeast coast with chilled-out beach vibe, Kumana bird sanctuary, and lagoon safaris.

River & Resort

Bentota

Bentota River Safari • Water Sports

Prime water sports hub featuring jet-skiing, river mangrove safaris, cinnamon island visits, and luxury beachfront hotels.

UNESCO Dutch Fort

Galle

Dutch Ramparts • Boutique Streets

Living 16th-century Dutch fortress with cobblestone alleys, lighthouse, artisan cafes, jewelry boutiques, and ocean ramparts.

Commercial Capital

Colombo

Galle Face Green • Lotus Tower

Vibrant seaside capital blending modern skyscrapers, colonial Portuguese & British heritage buildings, street markets, and fine dining.

Category 03

Hill Country & Highlands

Misty green mountain peaks, cascading waterfalls, tea factories, and panoramic train rides.

Little England

Nuwara Eliya

Tea Estates • Gregory Lake • Cool Climate

Highest town in Sri Lanka at 1,868m elevation, featuring cool crisp mountain weather, colonial bungalows, Hakgala Gardens, and Ceylon tea tasting.

Scenic Mountain Hub

Ella

Nine Arch Bridge • Little Adam's Peak

World-famous mountain village offering iconic blue train journeys over Nine Arch Bridge, Ravana waterfall, and hiking trails with Ella Gap vistas.

Category 04

Wildlife, Nature & Adventure

Jeep safaris, wild elephant herds, elusive leopards, white-water rafting, and rainforest biodiversity.

Eco Hub

Habarana

Minneriya Elephant Gathering • Eco Safaris

Gateway hub for Minneriya & Kaudulla national park safaris, famous for the annual Gathering of hundreds of wild Asian elephants.

Adventure Capital

Kitulgala

White-Water Rafting • Kelani River

Thrilling outdoor adventure zone set along Kelani River, featuring white-water rafting rapids, canyoning, and jungle trekking.

UNESCO Rainforest

Sinharaja

Primary Virgin Rainforest • Endemic Birds

Last viable area of primary tropical rainforest in Sri Lanka, boasting immense biodiversity with rare endemic flora and bird species.

Elephant Sanctuary

Udawalawe

Elephant Transit Home • Open Plains

Open grassland national park guaranteeing wild elephant sightings year-round along with water buffalo, deer, and raptors.

Natural Lakes

Wilpattu

Natural Villu Lakes • Sloth Bears & Leopards

Sri Lanka's largest national park famous for unique sand-rimmed natural rainfed lakes (Villus) and tranquil leopard tracking.

Leopard Capital

Yala National Park

Highest Leopard Density • Coastal Wilderness

Premier wildlife destination on the island boasting the world's highest density of wild Sri Lankan leopards, sloth bears, and crocodiles.
