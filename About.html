Our Journey & Values

Our Story: A Legacy of Travel

"A legacy of hospitality. A world of wonders."

Rooted in Passion, Growing Across Generations

Every unforgettable journey begins with a dream. For us, that dream is deeply rooted in a family passion for tourism that has grown across generations. From welcoming guests at local airports to curating luxury private tours, our commitment to genuine Sri Lankan hospitality has remained steadfast.

We believe discovering this beautiful island should be more than simply visiting places—it should be about experiencing its soul, connecting with its people, and carrying memories that last a lifetime.

Historical Evolution

The Evolution of Our Heritage

Scroll down to explore how decades of hands-on experience laid the foundation for Way2Wonders LK.

1978 Foundation

Lanka Metropolitan Tours & Travels

Our family's journey in the travel industry officially began, serving international travelers from around the world and establishing a strong foundation built on experience, reliability, and heartfelt Sri Lankan hospitality.

2016 Expansion

Sanaa International Tours

Continuing our passion, we expanded into specialized tourism, focusing on welcoming guests from Middle Eastern and global markets. This deepened our cultural understanding and refined our ability to create comfortable, highly personal itineraries.

2025 Present & Future

Way2Wonders LK Established

Inspired by 45+ years of family heritage, Way2Wonders LK was established in Kandy with a fresh, modern vision: uniting traditional values with modern personalized travel to show the world the wonders of Sri Lanka.
