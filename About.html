Who We Are

We are a professional property services company providing comprehensive cleaning, window cleaning, maintenance, superintendent services, post-construction cleaning, CCTV installation, renovation, landscaping, disinfection, and consultation services.

Since our founding in 2010, we've served thousands of residential and commercial clients across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area. Our focus is on delivering reliable, practical, and well-managed service solutions that exceed expectations.

Our team works responsibly and professionally to support the needs of property owners, managers, and businesses. We believe that excellent property maintenance shouldn't be complicated - it should be a smart choice.
