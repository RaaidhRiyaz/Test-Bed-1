About Way2Wonders LK

A Legacy of Travel. A New Way to Discover Sri Lanka.

Every unforgettable journey begins with a dream. Deeply rooted in a rich family passion for tourism that began in 1978 with Lanka Metropolitan Tours and evolved through Sanaa International Tours, our journey spans generations of dedication and authentic Sri Lankan warmth.

Established with a fresh vision, Way2Wonders LK brings together timeless knowledge with a modern, personal touch. From ancient UNESCO kingdoms and wildlife safaris to mist-covered tea hills and tranquil coastal escapes, we craft journeys that speak to your soul.
